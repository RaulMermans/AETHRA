# AETHRA · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 Presentation

Strict 7-slide, high-impact HTML slide deck designed specifically for the official 3-minute hackathon pitch and formatted for 16:9 PDF export.

## Quick Start

1. Open [`presentation/index.html`](index.html) in any browser.
2. **Keyboard Navigation**:
   - `Right Arrow` / `Space` / `Page Down`: Next slide.
   - `Left Arrow` / `Page Up`: Previous slide.
   - `Number keys 1 to 7`: Direct slide jump.
   - `N` or **Script**: Open the **Speaker Notes Drawer** with the exact spoken words and timing.
   - `G` or **Grid**: Toggle the **7-Slide Overview Grid**.
   - `F` or **Full**: Toggle Fullscreen.
   - `P` or **PDF**: Export the pixel-perfect 16:9 PDF.
   - `Esc`: Close open drawers.

## 16:9 PDF Export Instructions

1. Click **PDF** in the floating panel (or press **P** / **Cmd+P**).
2. Set **Destination** to *Save as PDF*, **Layout** to *Landscape*, **Margins** to *None*, and check **Background graphics**.
3. Save to produce a 7-page PDF.
