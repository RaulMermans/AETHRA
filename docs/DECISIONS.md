## Status vocabulary (from D33, proposed 14 Nov 2026)

- **LOCKED** — agreed and in force. Change only via an ADR plus team approval.
- **MVP DEFAULT** — what we use for the MVP. Change it only by recording the change here.
- **OPEN-P1** — not decided yet, or applies only after P0 is green.
- **DEFERRED** — out of scope for the hackathon (P2).
- **PROPOSED** — awaiting team approval. Not in force. **SUPERSEDED** — replaced.

Older rows (D1–D32) keep their original wording: **CURRENT** ≈ MVP DEFAULT and **OPEN** ≈
OPEN-P1. "UNDER REVIEW → Dxx" means a pending proposal would change that row.
